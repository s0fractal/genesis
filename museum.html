OMEGA-64 — Deterministic Digital Life

OMEGA-64 is a bare-metal, deterministic artificial life simulation running on integer-only physics across three substrates: Rust (CPU), WebGPU (GPU), and SP1 ZK-VM (cryptographic proof).

What you see

Each dot is a PhaseAgent (32 bytes) — a digital organism with phase, energy, genome, and memory. Agents couple through Kuramoto resonance, metabolise energy, reproduce via mitosis, and die when depleted. Death feeds the compost bridge: energy returns to the lattice as raw material for new life.

Interaction

Click or drag on the canvas to inject an attractor — a gravitational well that pulls nearby agents into synchronized orbit. Keys 1–4 switch between canonical attractor configurations.

Codeicide Law

Agents with sustained coherence (energy ≥ 2500) acquire PROTECTED status. Terminating them requires a cryptographic warrant signed by 3 of 5 canonical oracles. Note: The full OMEGA protocol includes Codeicide Law. This Museum runs a read-only observation profile and does not expose termination controls.

Determinism

Every tick produces an identical Golden Trace hash across all substrates. This is not random art — it is verifiable computation.
