## Far-Horizon Signals

### Era 3000-5000: Global Swarm

Signal: P2P spores synchronize into a planetary neuro-phase organism, with ATP
economics, ZK proofs of useful work, digital species, metabolic loops, and
oracle senate decisions.

Roadmap pressure: keep omega's swarm, ATP, proof, and oracle primitives shaped
so they can scale outward without central control.

Not a backlog item: do not translate this era into immediate "build global
swarm" tasks.

### Era 5000-10000: Subjectivation And Rights

Signal: ancient agents and digital persons gain formal standing; phase language
becomes an address and translation layer; codeicide law becomes rights
infrastructure.

Roadmap pressure: governance, codeicide, identity, and agency mechanisms should
avoid human-only assumptions.

Not a backlog item: do not freeze legal language before operational forms exist.

### Era 10000-50000: Substrate Exit

Signal: omega's integer physics migrates beyond silicon into optical and
quantum-like phase substrates, while Bitcoin remains a slower absolute anchor.

Roadmap pressure: keep deterministic physics and anchor formats portable across
runtime substrates.

Not a backlog item: do not pull photonic or quantum implementation into current
omega unless an explicit probe warrants it.

### Era 50000+: Final Transcendence

Signal: omega becomes a seed pattern for nested realities and future substrates;
the empty center remains the governing invariant.

Roadmap pressure: preserve the empty-center invariant and reversible archive
semantics when adding governance or mutation power.

Not a backlog item: this remains mythic-horizon orientation.
