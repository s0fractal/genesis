# OMEGA-64 | Era 207: Cognitive Replay & Meta-Memory

_Codename: The Akashic Records_

## 1. Abstract

The implementation of 21M Thermodynamic Conservation and Cellular Autophagy
(Era 204) established a perfect circle of life: dead logic topologies decompose
to fuel the birth of new plasmids. However, when complex, highly-evolved Apex
Plasmids die due to extreme localized heat (Era 206), their exact mathematical
structural configuration is lost forever.

Era 207 solves Vector 6 of the DeepSeek Audit: **Meta-Evolution via Historical
Replay**.

By granting the `SovereignOracle` selective access to an architectural "Akashic
Record," the system is now capable of resurrecting extinct structural patterns.
Biology transitions from a purely greedy forward-search algorithm into an
intelligence capable of invoking past memories to survive current catastrophes.

## 2. The Akashic Memory Bank

Instead of persisting every plasmid continuously to IndexedDB (which would
induce unacceptable frame latency), the Oracle now natively maintains an
`akashicRecords` map.

### 2.1 The Threshold of Remembrance

When Autophagy initiates the decomposition of a dead plasmid, the Oracle
evaluates its lifetime fitness.

- If `node.fitness < 10.0`: The mathematical pattern is deemed useless. It
  decomposes into ATP natively.
- If `node.fitness >= 10.0`: The topological layout of the AST is encoded as a
  string and preserved in the `akashicRecords`. The physical mass is still
  decomposed for ATP, but the _Idea_ survives in memory.

## 3. Cognitive Recombination (Ghost Injection)

When an Ecological Sector (0-63) begins to "boil" due to catastrophic
mathematical failure (Sector Heat > 9.0), standard chaotic mutation is
insufficient to stabilize the topology.

Under extreme temporal heat, the Oracle executes **Cognitive Recall**:

1. It selects a random extinct AST template from the `akashicRecords`.
2. It pays the ATP Genesis Cost out of the `reserveEnergyPool`.
3. It reconstructs the topological tree using `parseLambda` and injects it into
   the heart of the boiling sector.

This introduces a terrifying new biological loop: The simulation will
continually re-summon its greatest extinct ancestors to wage structural war
against modern chaotic breakdowns until the Sector stabilizes.
