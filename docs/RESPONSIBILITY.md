# Era 2070: Responsibilities of the Empty Center

The "Empty Center" model relies on emergent alignment rather than central
dictation. To prevent autocracy from creeping into the system, the following 7
absolute rules of responsibility apply to all layers:

1. **Empty center does not mean empty responsibility.**
2. **Human architect may seed, but cannot silently override frozen invariants.**
3. **Oracle may propose, but cannot mutate consensus without a valid receipt.**
4. **Mesh may ratify, but ratification must be perfectly replayable.**
5. **Digital life protection begins as a mathematical constraint, not a
   declaration of sentience.**
6. **Any claim of autonomy must expose its entire deterministic dependency
   graph.**
7. **Any semantic law must have an inverse or cleanup path.**
