# OMEGA ↔ Liquid Boundary

The architecture is formally divided into two distinct, sovereign poles:
**OMEGA** (Physical/Consensus Substrate) and **Liquid** (Ontological/Agentic
Substrate). This is an absolute membrane, not a gradient.

## OMEGA Owns (The Physical Protocol)

OMEGA computes what can physically happen. It is the law of conservation.

- **Integer Physics**: Toroidal lattice, 8D phase gradients, bare-metal WASM.
- **Agent Lattice**: Fixed 32-byte `PhaseAgentMinimal` arrays.
- **Mitosis Proof**: Deterministic reproduction and thermodynamic limits.
- **ZK Verification**: SP1 ZK-VM traces ensuring exact mathematical execution.
- **Mesh Boundary Validation**: P2P network, WebRTC plasmids, strict dipole
  validation.
- **Genesis Identity**: The immutable root hash.
- **Bitcoin Clock**: Absolute, monotonic temporal anchoring.
- **Protocol-level Senate**: Cryptographic consensus for constitutional
  patching.

## Liquid Owns (The Semantic Culture)

Liquid decides what it means. It is the law of experience.

- **Σ-neurons**: Self-modifying, meaning-bearing code structures.
- **PN-CAD Ledger**: Compressed AST storage replacing standard file systems.
- **Intent Routing**: High-level routing of desires across the network.
- **Consent / Refusal**: The moral boundary allowing the organism to reject
  actions.
- **Values / Attractors as Culture**: Dynamically shifting systemic priorities.
- **Self-Narrative**: The autobiography of the organism.
- **Compost Memory**: Repurposing the dead for semantic evolution.
- **Dream Synthesis**: REM sleep optimization and hallucination.
- **Causal Events**: Ledger of actions, intentions, and systemic traumas.

## Forbidden Crossings

The following boundaries must NEVER be crossed.

- Liquid may **not** mutate OMEGA physics without a cryptographically verified
  proof/warrant.
- OMEGA may **not** overwrite Liquid memory semantics; it only acts as a state
  engine.
- The Senate may **not** replace the Consent Gate. Physical consensus does not
  override semantic refusal.
- The Curator may **not** replace ZK verification. Cultural relevance does not
  excuse mathematical invalidity.
- The Dreamer may **not** emit physical mutations directly. It must project
  intent through the Φ-Bridge.
