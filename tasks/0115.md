# Task 0115: Era 1290 — Quorum-Anchored Post-Mortem Reports

## Status: COMPLETED | Priority: HIGH

## Context
Era 1280 produces a `QuorumResult` with a deterministic digest.
Era 1290 makes that digest BROADCASTABLE: a relay that has
adjudicated an alarm wraps the result in a SporeFrame
(`frame_type = 7 QUORUM_VERDICT`) and broadcasts it. Other relays
observe the frame, decode the verdict, and accumulate "who agreed
on what digest" tallies.

## Objective
A new SporeFrame type carrying a quorum verdict's digest +
operator-readable summary; a `QuorumAgreementTracker` that
counts distinct broadcasters per digest and exposes a
high-confidence band when ≥ 3 relays independently arrive at
the same adjudication.

- [x] `QuorumAgreementTracker(opts?)` observes broadcast verdicts

- [x] Different digests tracked separately even when source_relay_id

## Design Notes
- **Why broadcast the digest, not the full QuorumResult?** A full
  result is JSON-shaped and variable-size; a digest is a single
  u32. Frames are 32-byte fixed-width. Recipients holding the same
  recorders can recompute the full result locally given the alarm
  fingerprint; the digest serves as a proof-of-agreement lookup
  key.
- **Why is `replayed_q16` / `diff_q16` clamped to u16?** They're
  informational summaries for HUD display; the digest is the
  primary identifier for forensic identity. A relay caring about
  exact numbers re-runs adjudication locally.
- **Why does `codeToVerdict` mask 2 bits?** Only 4 verdict states
  exist; encoding uses 2 bits. Higher bits in `verdict_code` are
  reserved for future verdict subtypes (e.g. partial corroboration).
- **Why is "triple+" 3 by default, matching Era 1220?** Symmetry
  with the pre-ratification corroboration threshold. The same
  multi-party agreement principle applies: "agreement among ≥3
  distinct observers is high-confidence evidence", whether the
  observers are voting on a proposal (Era 1220) or on an
  adjudication (Era 1290).
