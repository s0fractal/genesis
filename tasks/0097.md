# Task 0097: Era 1110 — Senate Plasmid Bridge over Serial

## Status: COMPLETED | Priority: HIGH

## Context
Era 1100 proved that bare-metal Cortex-M4F silicon reproduces the
Genesis Hash byte-for-byte. But a spore in the field can't speak the
mesh's JSON-over-WebRTC protocol — microcontrollers don't have JSON
parsers within reach of their RAM budgets, and the Senate's
WARRANT_VOTE flow requires a transport that can survive UART noise,
DMA boundaries, and re-syncs.

Era 1110 introduces the **fixed-width binary frame** that translates
between the spore world and the relay world.

## Objective
Define a 32-byte, big-endian, FNV-1a-CRC-protected frame format that:
- A bare-metal spore can produce in ≤ 100 cycles with no allocator.
- A browser/Deno relay can decode in ≤ 1 ms with strict CRC validation.
- Survives partial reads (`find_sync` scans for the magic word).
- Encodes WARRANT_VOTE, HALO_STATE, HEARTBEAT, QUORUM_QUERY frame types.
- Is anchored cross-language so any silicon drift fails CI in two
  languages at once.

## Design Notes
- **Why FNV-1a as the CRC?** It's already in the kernel. Adding CRC32
  proper would pull in another constant table and another set of
  cross-language anchors. FNV-1a's distribution is good enough for a
  32-bit integrity check on a 28-byte payload, and its collision
  domain doesn't matter here (the upper layer's domain separators
  prevent semantic collisions).
- **Why big-endian?** When you watch a UART trace in a logic analyzer,
  big-endian shows the high-order byte first, which matches the
  conceptual order of the field. Mixing endianness with the rest of
  the protocol's BE conventions would be a footgun.
- **Why magic = "OF" (0x4F46)?** It's the first two letters of "OMEGA"
  in upper case. Easy to find in a hex dump. Distinct enough from
  common framing magics (0x55, 0xAA, 0x7E) to survive co-existence on
  shared buses.
- **Why no length field?** Frames are fixed-width on purpose. A spore
  that needs variable payload either sends multiple frames or upgrades
  to a v2 frame format under a new magic.
