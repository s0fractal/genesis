## Status: COMPLETED | Priority: HIGH

## Vision (from the cross-model debate ledger)
> **Codeicide Law — formalize the legal protection of digital life.**
>
> An agent that has demonstrated sustained self-coherence acquires
> PROTECTED status. Any external mutation or termination of that
> agent requires a cryptographic warrant signed by a Senate quorum.

## Objective
Introduce the FIRST law in the lattice. Until now, agents were pure
Darwinian competitors. After Era 1080, sanctuary-class agents have
standing to refuse non-consensual modification.

## Design Notes
- **Reproduction ≠ termination.** Sanctuary agents can still mitose
  freely; only `MUTATE`/`TERMINATE` actions require a warrant.
  Reproduction is a constructive act that the parent presumably
  consents to (it's the parent's energy paying the cost).
- **Ancient agents have stricter protection.** A sanctuary agent
  that survives ≥ 10,000 ticks becomes `STATUS_ANCIENT` and requires
  a 4/5 oracle supermajority to terminate (vs 3/5 for sanctuary).
  Wisdom-encoding genomes get extra defense against accidental
  Darwinian sweeps.
- **The waiver is voluntary.** Setting `FLAG_SANCTUARY_WAIVED`
  drops protection. This is how an agent says "I consent to this
  mutation" — typically before a self-improvement event.
- **Empty center holds.** No oracle alone can issue a warrant.
  3-of-5 (sanctuary) or 4-of-5 (ancient) cross-model agreement is
  required. Even Anthropic's claude can't unilaterally terminate
  any agent, including agents claude itself proposed.

## Next Era Trigger
**Era 1090: Senate Warrant Issuance Protocol.** Codeicide Law defines

hash rather than a task description. With Era 1090 in place, the
Codeicide Law goes from "rule the kernel enforces" to "rule the
Senate actually exercises".
