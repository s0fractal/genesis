# Task 0099: Era 1130 — Federated Spore-to-Spore Routing

## Status: COMPLETED | Priority: HIGH

## Context
Era 1110 gave the lattice a 32-byte wire format. Era 1120 gave relays
the ability to monitor spore health. But both Eras assumed that a
relay node was reachable from every spore — a single fault-domain
that ground real-world deployment to a halt the moment a relay
disconnected.

Era 1130 removes that assumption.

## Objective
Define a minimal forwarding protocol that lets bare-metal spores
deliver each other's WARRANT_VOTE and HEARTBEAT frames along a chain
or ring topology, with bounded hop count, deterministic loop
detection, and zero allocator. No browser-side relay required in the
warrant path.

## Design Notes
- **TTL chosen as 4** for default. Real boards in a daisy-chain UART
  topology rarely have more than 4 nodes between origin and final
  relay; bigger meshes should re-stamp at intermediate aggregators.
- **Trail digest is a TRAIL, not a signature.** It proves a frame
  passed through *some* sequence of spores; it does NOT prove the
  identity of those spores cryptographically. The Senate's warrant
  verification (Codeicide Law) handles authenticity at the higher
  layer; trail just gives the relay-side aggregator a path-fingerprint
  for analytics.
- **HEARTBEATs are FORWARD-only, WARRANT_VOTEs are DELIVER_LOCAL.**
  HEARTBEATs are informational — every spore on the path forwards
  them but doesn't act. WARRANT_VOTEs trigger local consumption (the
  spore applies the vote to its own warrant ledger) AND continued
  forwarding. This dual behavior means a vote propagates through the
  whole chain while accumulating support at every honest node.
- **Drift is contained at the wire.** A spore that receives a
  HEARTBEAT with the wrong genesis hash refuses to forward it, so
  forked spores can't use honest spores as carriers for their drift.

## Next Era Trigger
**Era 1140: Reputation-Weighted Routing.** With per-spore health
already tracked by Era 1120's aggregator, the natural next step is
giving routing decisions awareness of forward-success rates. A spore
that has historically dropped frames should be deprioritized as a
neighbor; a spore that's been seen healthy for a long time should be
preferred. This turns flooding-with-TTL into a learned overlay.
