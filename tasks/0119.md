# Task 0119: Era 1330 — Sync Scheduler + Retransmission Driver

## Status: COMPLETED | Priority: HIGH

## Context
Era 1320 chunks an `ArchiveDelta` into a sequence of SporeFrames and
reassembles them. But it's synchronous (caller drives the whole
envelope in one shot) and assumes a perfect link. Production needs:
- *when* to initiate a sync per peer (periodic, with backoff on failure);
- recovery from individual frame loss (selectively retransmit, not
  re-ship the whole envelope);
- a give-up horizon so a partner that drops every retransmit doesn't
  consume the receiver's resources forever.

## Objective
Provide pure decision-making layers on top of Era 1320:
1. **Scheduler** — per-peer state with shouldSyncNow / record outcome
   helpers; exponential backoff on failure with hard cap.
2. **Retransmission Driver** — per-envelope state accumulating frames;
   `decideAction` returns `complete | retransmit | wait | giveup`.

All functions are pure: caller provides `now_ms` and config.

### 3. Action Decision ✅
- [x] `decideAction(env, config, now_ms)` returns:
  - `{kind:"complete", delta}` when reassembleDelta succeeds.
  - `{kind:"retransmit", sequences}` when missing sequences exist
    AND none have exhausted retries AND cooldown elapsed.
  - `{kind:"wait", reason}` when cooldown is active.
  - `{kind:"giveup", reason, partial_frame_count}` when:
    - total time exceeds `envelope_giveup_ms`, or
    - every missing sequence is over `max_attempts_per_sequence`, or
    - reassembleDelta returned a non-missing-sequence error.

  `max_attempts_per_sequence`, sequence joins `abandoned_sequences`.
- [x] Abandoned sequences are excluded from future retransmit lists.
- [x] Cooldown updates `last_retransmit_request_ms`.

## Design Notes
- **Why exponential backoff with multiplicative folding?** The intent
  is: after the n-th consecutive failure, the next-try delay should
  be `base × multiplier^n`, capped at the ceiling. Straightforward
  iterative loop preserves the cap semantics and matches what
  operators expect from "every retry doubles the wait".
- **Why per-sequence attempt cap, not just envelope-wide?** Realistic
  loss patterns are bursty in *which* frame goes missing, but a
  specific frame may be permanently undeliverable (a router silently
  drops it, a sender's buffer bug). The driver should keep trying
  the *other* missing frames as long as forward progress is
  possible, only surrendering the envelope when no eligible
  sequences remain. This prevents one bad sequence from blocking
  recovery of the rest.
- **Why pure functions?** Memory cost: a relay tracking N peers
  with M in-flight envelopes shouldn't accumulate hidden state via
  closures. Plus tests need to reproduce exact backoff curves and
  cooldown windows without sleeping or mocking timers.
- **Why surface `partial_frame_count` in giveup?** Diagnostics. An
  operator looking at why an envelope was abandoned wants to know
  "was the link basically working (partial count near total) or
  completely dead (0 frames received)?". Caller can log this for
  fleet-wide loss-pattern analysis.
- **Why no jitter in scheduler?** Era 1330 is the bare minimum
  decision layer. Jitter belongs in a higher coordinator (Era 1340)
  that owns multiple peers and can stagger their syncs to avoid
  thundering-herd on shared network resources.

## Next Era Trigger
**Era 1340: Multi-Peer Sync Coordinator.** Era 1330 handles a single
peer + a single envelope. Production has N peers and possibly
parallel in-flight envelopes per peer. Era 1340 will provide the
top-level coordinator that:
- iterates `shouldSyncNow` across all known peers, picking the next
  initiator based on reputation × estimated archive divergence;
- deduplicates identical envelopes arriving from different sources
  (e.g. peer A and peer B both sending the same delta we requested
  from one);
- exposes a fleet-wide "convergence rate" metric — what fraction of
  the network's known digests has each relay observed?
- bridges back into the rest of the OMEGA stack via a periodic
  COMPOSITE_HEALTH update.
