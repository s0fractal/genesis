# Task 0106: Era 1200 — Snapshot-as-Plasmid + Peer Partition Monitor

## Status: COMPLETED | Priority: HIGH

## Context
Era 1190 made resilience metrics serializable as a 32-byte
ResilienceSnapshot. But the SporeFrame transport (Era 1110) is
itself 32 bytes; the full snapshot doesn't fit inside one. Era 1200
solves this with a **compact digest** carried in SporeFrame's
existing payload slots, plus a destination-side `PeerSnapshotMonitor`
that compares peer digests against the local detector and surfaces
partition alarms.

## Objective
Wrap the headline resilience numbers (relay_id, total_intents,
double_witness, redundancy_rate_q16, tick) into a SporeFrame
payload — frame_type 5 = `SNAPSHOT_DIGEST`. Every relay periodically
broadcasts its digest; every relay observes peers' digests and
triggers a partition alarm when measurements diverge by ≥10%.

## Design Notes
- **Why a digest instead of full snapshot wrapping?** A 32-byte
  ResilienceSnapshot doesn't fit inside a 32-byte SporeFrame
  payload (which has only 24 bytes available for content after
  magic + type + oracle_bit + CRC). A compact digest carrying the
  five most operationally useful numbers fits easily. Full
  snapshots can still be broadcast separately when bandwidth
  allows (raw 32-byte transport, magic "RS").
- **Why reconstruct `single_witness = total - double`?** The digest
  doesn't carry triple+ counts (rare in practice). Approximating
  single = total - double overestimates single by however many
  triples there are — but that only makes partition detection
  slightly more conservative, which is fine.
- **Why require both sides non-zero for alarm?** Otherwise a relay
  that just rebooted (empty detector) would constantly trigger
  alarms against any healthy peer. The observer needs its own
  baseline before judging others.
- **The on_alarm callback is the integration hook for Era 1090.**
  An alarm can auto-raise a `WARRANT_PROPOSAL` with action_code 3
  (RELOCATE) targeting the suspected relay's identity, asking
  three oracles to ratify an investigation. Wiring that auto-raise
  is left to deployment-specific code; the monitor just emits.
