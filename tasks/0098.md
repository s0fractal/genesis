# Task 0098: Era 1120 — Liveness Telemetry Aggregator

## Status: COMPLETED | Priority: HIGH

## Context
Era 1110 gave the lattice a binary wire format (32-byte FNV-1a-CRC
frames). But emitting frames is half a transport — receiving them and
classifying spore health is the other half. Without operational
visibility, a fleet of $5 spores in the field becomes a black box: any
node could be stalled, forked, or silent and you'd never know.

Era 1120 closes that loop.

## Objective
Provide a relay-side observer that consumes HEARTBEAT and WARRANT_VOTE
frames, tracks each spore's tick advancement and last-seen wall-clock,
and classifies every spore into one of five health states with O(1)
per-frame cost.

### 2. Health Classes ✅

- [x] **stalled**: seen recently, multiple heartbeats with same tick.
- [x] **forked**: claimed genesis ≠ canonical v1.0 — non-conforming impl.
  FORKED dominates LOST (drift > silence).
- [x] **lost**: no frame for ≥ `maxSilenceMs` (default 30 s).
- [x] **unknown**: ≥1 sample but below `classifyAfter` threshold.

## Design Notes
- **The aggregator does NOT trust spore identity.** A spore that
  broadcasts a wrong genesis hash is FORKED, not "impostor". The
  Senate's authentication layer (Codeicide warrants + dipole
  invariant) handles authenticity at higher layers; this module just
  reports what it observes.
- **stallTicks defaults to 0** — any spore that fails to advance its
  tick across consecutive heartbeats is stalled. Real boards advance
  on every cycle; if they don't, something is wrong.
- **delta self-test classifies as STALLED** even though it sent
  warrant votes — that's intentional. The aggregator wants to see
  fresh HEARTBEAT frames with rising tick. A spore that votes but
  doesn't heartbeat is suspect; relay-level vigilance, not
  protocol-level rejection.
