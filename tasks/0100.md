# Task 0100: Era 1140 — Reputation-Weighted Routing

## Status: COMPLETED | Priority: HIGH

## Context
Era 1130 gave the lattice TTL-bounded peer-to-peer flooding — every
spore on the path forwards every frame. That works for daisy-chain
topologies, but in BLE-mesh / UART-star deployments where a node has
multiple neighbors, blind flooding wastes radio energy and tangles
the trail digest.

Era 1140 introduces a learned overlay: routing decisions consult the
Era 1120 LivenessAggregator's per-neighbor health metrics and pick
the best k forwarding candidates instead of broadcasting to all.

## Objective
A deterministic reputation function that converts each tracked
neighbor's `SporeRecord` into a numeric score, with hard exclusion
for forked spores and graceful degradation for stalled / lost ones.
Two relays observing the same frame history MUST produce identical
rankings.

## Design Notes
- **Forked is a HARD exclusion.** This is the same invariant as
  Era 1130's wire-side drift containment: anchor mismatch is loud
  enough to be cryptographically rejected, not soft-penalized.
  Drift propagates nothing — neither frames nor reputation.
- **Heartbeat density caps at 50** to prevent a chatty spore from
  dominating routing forever. After 50 heartbeats, your reputation
  growth is capped — you maintain it by staying healthy, not by
  generating more beacons.
- **Determinism matters.** Two relays in different parts of the
  network must produce the same ranking from the same observation
  history, otherwise their forwarding decisions diverge and the
  mesh fragments. The tie-break by spore_id is part of the
  determinism contract.
- **The aggregator does the sweep.** `rankNeighbors` always calls
  `aggregator.sweep(now_ms)` first, so silence-induced LOST
  classifications are evaluated against the same `now_ms` as the
  scoring. Otherwise stale `health` fields would lie.

## Next Era Trigger
**Era 1150: Adaptive TTL.** With reputation scores quantifying
neighbor reliability, the routing layer can stop using a fixed
`DEFAULT_TTL = 4` and instead compute per-frame TTL as a function
of the path's expected reliability. A chain through 4 healthy
neighbors might warrant TTL=2 (less margin needed); a chain through
mixed-health neighbors warrants TTL=8 (more retries headroom).
