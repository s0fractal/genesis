# Task 0116: Era 1300 — Verdict Persistence + Cold Archive

## Status: COMPLETED | Priority: HIGH

## Context
Era 1290's `QuorumAgreementTracker` is in-memory and FIFO-bounded.
Verdicts older than the eviction window disappear. For long-term
audit trails, operators need cold storage: a deterministic
import/export format that survives process restarts.

## Objective
Define a newline-delimited JSON archive format for high-confidence
verdicts. Export from a `QuorumAgreementTracker`, import with
integrity verification (archive_hash drift = tampering detected),
diff two archives by digest set membership.

## Design Notes
- **Why ND-JSON instead of single-blob JSON?** Streaming
  imports can validate manifest first, then stream records
  one-line-at-a-time without loading the entire file. Useful
  for archives with millions of verdicts on disk.
- **Why `only_high_confidence: true` by default?** Operators
  building long-term audit trails want adjudication-grade
  evidence, not exploratory records. Lower-confidence records
  are still inspectable in-memory; the archive is intentionally
  filtered.
- **Why is `archive_hash` over digests only, not full records?**
  Two archivists may have observed slightly different
  metadata (different broadcaster IDs in `adjudicators`, etc.)
  but agree on the digest set. The hash captures the
  "we agreed these are the verdicts" claim; record-by-record
  comparison is for `diffArchives` operator workflows.
- **Why bundle the manifest as line 0 of ND-JSON?** Allows
  the importer to verify schema/hash before even parsing
  records — early-exit on tampered files saves I/O.
